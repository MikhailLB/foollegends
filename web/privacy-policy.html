Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fool Legends (the "Game", package com.legendfool.foollegends) handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Fool Legends does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The Game does not require an account, login, or registration.

2. Local Data

Your best level (high score) is stored only on your device using local storage and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game removes this data.

3. Permissions

The Game may use the device vibration motor for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

4. Third Parties

The Game contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not share data with third parties.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@foollegends.com.
