Fool Legends — Support

Welcome to Fool Legends, the reaction & memory game where the Joker keeps breaking the rules. Need help? You're in the right place.

How to Play

The Joker shows a command, e.g. "Press red". Tap the matching color.

But beware: when the Joker says "I'm lying", you must press anything but the named color.

In later levels the Joker flashes a series of colors to repeat — and when he lies, you must press the opposite colors (red ↔ blue, green ↔ yellow). A timer is added as you progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

My progress reset. Best level is stored locally; reinstalling or clearing app data removes it.
